# Code of Conduct

Mangrove Atlas follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). Everyone taking part in this project's issues, pull requests, discussions and events agrees to it.

## In short

- Be respectful and welcoming, whatever someone's background, identity, experience or field.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Assume good intent, and ask before assuming.
- Respect local and Indigenous knowledge of coastal places. When people share it, credit them.
- Harassment, personal attacks, sexualised language or imagery, doxxing and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

## Reporting

To report a problem, contact a maintainer privately. Message a member of the [Flora-Carbon-AI organisation](https://github.com/Flora-Carbon-AI) on GitHub, or use GitHub's "Report content" option on the comment or issue. Reports are handled confidentially.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, or temporarily or permanently ban contributors, as described in the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines.
